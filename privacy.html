Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the information you choose to share: your name, email address, phone number, and your answers and message about your real estate needs.

How it’s used

Your information is used only to reply to your inquiry and help with your real estate needs. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

How the form works

Contact-form messages are delivered to Millie by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service. Its handling of the data it delivers is described in its own privacy policy. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security. The Market Trends page loads public data only and does not track you.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you’ve sent, or to stop contacting you, at any time.

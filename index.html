Meet Millie

Millie Killpack

Real estate agent · Ivie Avenue Real Estate, LLC

Hi, I’m Millie. Since 2006, I’ve helped more than 200 families buy, sell, and build homes along the Wasatch Front. Almost every one of them found me the same way: a friend or family member who worked with me first. No billboards, no cold calls, no dancing TikToks. Just good work and happy clients.

I’m not your typical agent. I drive a minivan, not a luxury car, and I’d rather put your time and money toward what actually helps you. That means returning calls quickly, telling you the truth, and negotiating hard for you, while knowing when a compromise will close the deal. I love getting a home ready to shine before it hits the market. If you’re building, I’ll sit in on design meetings and watch for builder upcharges so you don’t have to.

Lehi is home. I graduated from BYU, and today my husband and I are raising our four kids here, so I know how much the right neighborhood matters.

Whether it’s your first home or your fifth, I’d love to help. Let’s connect.
